The Legend of Zelda \[PAL\]

[color=red]Story
[color=white]The kingdom of Hyrule has fallen
into chaos after the evil Prince
of Darkness, Ganon, invaded and
stole the Triforce of Power.
To protect the land, 
Princess Zelda split the
Triforce of Wisdom into eight
fragments and hid them across
secret underworld dungeons
before being captured.
A young traveler named Link 
comes to the aid of Zelda's
nursemaid, Impa, and embarks on 
a dangerous quest across Hyrule.
To defeat Ganon and rescue the
Princess, Link must conquer each
dungeon, recover all eight 
fragments, and assemble 
the Triforce of Wisdom.
